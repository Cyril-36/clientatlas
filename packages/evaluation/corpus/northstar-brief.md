# Northstar Labs Onboarding Brief

Northstar Labs is a fictional software company used only for the ClientAtlas
portfolio demonstration. Its onboarding objective is to migrate customer
onboarding to a self-service portal.

The executive sponsor is Mira Chen. The implementation owner is Avery Stone.
The target launch date is 15 March 2027. The steering committee meets every
Tuesday.

Success means reducing onboarding time-to-value by 30 percent and reaching
80 percent portal activation within sixty days. The largest known delivery risk
is delayed access to the legacy CRM export.
