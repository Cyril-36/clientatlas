# Demonstration Video Script

Target length: four to six minutes.

1. Show the backend-only boundary and architecture.
2. Authenticate and open the synthetic Northstar workspace.
3. Upload a DOCX and show every ingestion state.
4. Ask an answerable question and open its citation.
5. Ask an unanswerable question and show abstention.
6. Show malicious document text being treated as untrusted evidence.
7. Generate and edit the Readiness Report, then show immutable history.
8. Delete a source and show evidence marked for review.
9. Show evaluation results and telemetry.
10. End with cross-tenant and CI test evidence.
